Rail × Island

Three ways to give the Rail the Island's look and alerts

All three hybrids use the Island's material (near-black, fully round, hairline edge, blur-morph transitions) and its alert system (same priority rules, about 3s on screen, a glow in the event's colour, View). They differ in where an alert appears and how detail opens.

Concept Reserved Alerts appear Detail opens as Notes Watch out for

My pick: 4 · Crown. It keeps the Island's alert system almost unchanged (one fixed spot, queued, same morph and glow) on top of the Rail's always-visible icons. Clicking turns crown and rail into one island card instead of a separate flyout. If the 372px card feels too heavy, 3 · Capsule is the conservative option: your Rail, just rounder and darker, with alerts that slide out of the icon they belong to.
