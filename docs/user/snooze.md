# Snooze

Snooze hides a thread from the Inbox until a time you pick. It moves to the **Snoozed** section and
comes back on its own. A thread also comes back early when it needs you: a request for approval or
an answer, a failed run, a finished turn, or a message you send to it.

## Choosing the time

Open a thread's menu in the Inbox (or select several threads) and choose **Snooze**:

- **In 1 hour** and **In 3 hours** count from when you choose them.
- **This evening** (18:00, offered until then), **Tomorrow morning** (9:00) and **Next week**
  (Monday 9:00) use your local time.
- **Custom…** asks for a date and time in your local time zone, or a duration in minutes, hours or
  days. A duration starts when you confirm; one day is 24 hours.

The command palette's **Snooze until** view (also the snooze shortcut) offers the same choices.
**Unsnooze** brings a snoozed thread back right away. Snooze has an Undo notice; see
[Sidebar Undo](../sidebar-undo.md).

## While a thread is snoozed

Opening a snoozed thread shows a notice above the composer: when it returns, with a countdown
(`Returns Thu, Oct 30, 16:00 · 1 hr 28 min left`), and that sending a message ends the snooze.
**Return now** wakes it, and **Change time** snoozes it again until a preset or a custom time.

## Woke

When a snooze runs out, the thread's Inbox row says **Woke** (with an alarm clock) until you open
it on that device. A request, a failure or running work shows instead while it lasts. A thread that
came back early shows why it did (it needs input, failed or finished), not Woke.

## On the iPhone app

The Inbox row's long-press menu has the same presets and **Custom…**, which picks a day and a time
in 15-minute steps, or a duration. Rows say **Woke** the same way; opening the thread clears it on
the phone.
